## Notes

- The "5 false positives" on the frozen-148 A/B are pre-existing and unchanged:
  they are B-category ("related but different fact") pairs that legitimately share
  an **event** but not a **claim**; `eval-identity.ts`'s `differentPairs()`
  excludes B-category for this reason, the coarser `ab()` A/B does not. The real
  precision gates — `eval:claims` claim-matching precision (100%) and the
  false-corroboration rate (0/71) — are clean.
- The one remaining corpus failure is `H07` (a prediction whose attribution is
  not retained). It is a prediction/attribution-extraction gap, **not** an
  event- or claim-identity miss, and was explicitly out of scope for v0.6.
- Attribution retention is 96.2% (25/26) — unchanged; the single miss is `H07`.
- No decision-gate confidence threshold was lowered. Candidate generation was
  made more generous (a distinctive-quantity block; a state-level ≥2-shared-
  concept block; order-independent concept counting). The gate still decides.
