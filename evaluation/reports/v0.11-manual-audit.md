## Findings

- **F1 — Framing emphasis is the weakest signal.** ~72% of sampled headlines came back `uncategorised` (question / quote / list / subordinate-clause structure). Matches `eval:framing` recall of 41%. The story-page **Headlines** tab is thin because of this.
- **F2 — Tamil clusters are the worst-served.** Stance and emphasis lexicons are English-first; an all-Tamil cluster (e.g. `20-1ohpzc`, the new Secretariat) shows mostly `unclear`. The v0.11 Tamil *volume* fix (4.5% → 16%) is not matched by a Tamil *analysis* fix.
- **F3 — SOURCE_FAMILY / OWNERSHIP blindspots produce noise** on routine sports / other-relevant content where family concentration is normal (`tilak-...-duleep-trophy`: 9 articles, mostly Kasturi & Sons — flagged CLEAR_ASYMMETRY, but unremarkable). Gate these to consequential stories.
- **F4 — Generic-title CAP clustering.** SACHET alerts titled "Very Heavy Rain" still over-merge across districts.
- **F5 — Category classifier mis-files** (a NEET exam story → `finance`). Known RC-1 issue.
- **F6 — Ownership registry + source-family counts held up** — **0 errors of 18.** The v0.10/v0.11 registry work is solid.

## What this audit changes for v0.11 / v0.12

1. **Do not claim framing accuracy.** `eval:framing` + this audit both say the emphasis detector fires on well under half of real headlines. The Headlines tab ships with that caveat.
2. **Tamil analysis parity** is a top v0.12 item — the volume is there now, the NLP is not.
3. **Blindspot gating** should add a consequence filter for SOURCE_FAMILY / OWNERSHIP types (v0.12).
4. **Ownership and independence counting are trustworthy** — safe to keep prominent.
