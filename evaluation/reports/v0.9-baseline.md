The trend score conflates **publication velocity** (noisy on the 15-min cadence — a wire story
landing reads as "5–10× acceleration") and **publication recency** with **editorial
importance**. Measured over the 30 trending clusters:

## Root diagnosis → v0.9 editorial model

The fix is **not** classification (52% other-relevant is close to the legitimate floor). It is a
**separate, interpretable EditorialPriority layer** that re-ranks with:

- **geoRelevance** — P0 a strong multiplier, not a small additive bump
- **informationGain** — a `duplicate` / `more-of-same` update is heavily penalised for
  *prominence* (it can still be listed under "more")
- **consequence** — a soft feature must not out-rank a public-safety event
- **independent corroboration** — families, not raw report count; syndication penalised
- **meaningful-update recency** — time since a fact changed, not time since a wire pickup
- **generic-CAP penalty** — a titleless "Heavy Rain" watch with 1 family and no district is
  BACKGROUND, not trending
- **source-concentration control** — one publisher must not fill the homepage

Then **editorial bands** — `URGENT / HIGH / STANDARD / BACKGROUND / SUPPRESSED` — and a home
page that surfaces by band. OTHER_RELEVANT flows to BACKGROUND / "More", de-emphasised
editorially, not misclassified.
